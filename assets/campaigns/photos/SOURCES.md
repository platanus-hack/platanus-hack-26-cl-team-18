# Photo sources

Fictional-catalog photos (`energy`, `coffee`, `beer`, `rayo`, `energy-coffee`,
`rayo-coffee`) are unbranded AI scenery; prompts in `../prompts.json`.

Chile catalog (`DEMO_CATALOG=chile`), built by `scripts/build_chile_photos.py`:

- `cl-score`, `cl-red-bull`, `cl-monster`, `cl-score-cafe`, `cl-red-bull-cafe`: the
  unbranded studio shots above with the can's rim light moved to the brand's hue.
  No mark, label or packaging is drawn (CLAUDE.md invariant 4).
- Real photos from Wikimedia Commons (Chilean counter food, drinks and sweets,
  Ruta 5 and a Copec station), credited below as the licenses require. Food,
  drink and road only: no recognisable faces, no third-party marks. CC BY-SA
  derivatives (the crops) stay under CC BY-SA. They air in rotation and as
  category scenes (manifest `alternates`, `category:<name>`).

Team-supplied campaign references (WhatsApp, 2026-11-22), framed by the same helpers
(16:9 crop or pack shot) and shown only for their own brand: real product photography
with the brand's own packaging, never edited or redrawn (CLAUDE.md invariant 4).
`cl-domino-completos` (Dominó), `cl-starbucks-frappuccino` (Starbucks),
`cl-guinness-pint` (Guinness 0.0), `cl-monster-hielo` (Monster) and `cl-vital-picnic`
(Vital). Trademarks of their owners, used for a hackathon demo without affiliation.
